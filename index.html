Crowdsourced Personalization

About

AI chatbots isolate us into individual experiences. Sometimes, those experiences aren’t made equal.

We’re studying how chatbots personalize responses to their users. We’re trying to spot issues like political echo chambers, leading users astray by always agreeing with them, or some users getting worse responses than others.

To do this, we’re making a tool that lets *anyone* compare the responses they’re getting to what everyone else is getting. By using our browser extension, you can find out how your experience using AI compares to others’. By sharing your responses, you’ll also contribute to research working to make sure AI responses are safe and fair for everyone, and hold systems accountable when they aren’t.
